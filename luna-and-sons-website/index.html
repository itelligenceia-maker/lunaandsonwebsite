About Us

A family name on every yard we touch.

Luna & Sons Landscaping has been family-owned and operated in Santa Maria since 2019. It started small — one truck, a trailer of hand tools, and a handful of neighborhood lawns — run by a father and his two sons who are still out on job sites today. Word spread yard by yard, and what began as weekend mowing and cleanup work grew into a full-service landscaping company serving homeowners and businesses across the Central Coast.

The way we work hasn't changed since that first truck. We show up when we say we will, we build things that last, and we stand behind every project with our own name — because it is our name, not a franchise or a rotating crew of subcontractors. The same team that walks your property with you on day one is the same team that finishes the job.

We're fully licensed and insured (License #45178), so every design, hardscape, and irrigation job is backed by real accountability — not just a handshake. And because we've spent years working Central Coast soil and weather, we know what actually thrives here, from drought-tolerant plantings to irrigation systems built around our water restrictions — not just what looks good on paper.
