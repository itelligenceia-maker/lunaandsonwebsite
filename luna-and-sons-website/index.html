About Us

A family name on every yard we touch.

Luna & Sons is a father-and-sons crew — the same people who show up on day one are the same ones who finish the job. We believe good landscaping isn't just about how a yard looks on installation day, it's about whether it still holds up years later, which is why every plant, pathway, and irrigation line we install is chosen for how it performs in Central Coast conditions, not just how it photographs.

We're fully licensed and insured (License #45178), and we treat every property — a small front yard refresh or a full install — with the same level of care. No subcontractors, no rotating crews, just the people who built this company from one truck in 2019.
