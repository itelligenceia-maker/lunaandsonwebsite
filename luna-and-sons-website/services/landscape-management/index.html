Landscape Management

Ongoing care to keep your landscape looking its best, year-round.

Protect the landscape you’ve invested in.

A beautiful landscape takes ongoing care to stay healthy, clean, and looking its best. Luna & Sons provides dependable landscape maintenance tailored to your property, from routine upkeep and seasonal cleanups to planting, irrigation checks, and general landscape care.

We focus on the details that keep your outdoor space looking polished throughout the year, so you can enjoy your property without worrying about the constant upkeep.

Landscape Management

Every property has different needs. Our landscape management services are built around your property, your priorities, and the level of care it requires.

We can handle routine mowing and trimming, seasonal planting and refreshes, irrigation checks and minor repairs, fertilization, cleanup, and general landscape upkeep. Rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach, we evaluate the property and recommend the work needed to keep it healthy and well maintained.

Reliable Maintenance, Without the Guesswork

Whether you need ongoing landscape maintenance or help getting an overgrown property back under control, our team can build a maintenance plan around your needs.

As a family-owned company, Luna & Sons takes pride in doing the small things right—from clean edges and tidy beds to consistent communication and leaving the property clean when the work is finished. Our goal is simple: dependable service and a landscape you're proud to come home to.
